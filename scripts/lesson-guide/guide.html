Where to meet

Adult group lessons meet at the base of Explorer Gondola in Mountain Village. For private lessons, follow your instructor?s instructions; the default meeting spot is the black private lessons flag in front of the Mountain Sports Office.

Montage and Spanish Peaks bookings are for Montage lodging guests and Spanish Peaks members.

Lift access and equipment are separate.Each guest needs a valid lift ticket or pass and rental equipment if needed.
Lunch and reservationsFull-day winter group lessons include lunch; arrange your own for private and half-day lessons. Bring nut-free snacks and share dietary restrictions at checkout. Book online or call Mountain Sports at 800-548-4486 after coordinating plans with Carter.
